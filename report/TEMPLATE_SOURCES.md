# Report format and emblem provenance

Reviewed on 3 November 2026.

## IIT Kharagpur templates reviewed

- MUST Lab, IIT Kharagpur, publicly provided LaTeX templates: https://www.mustlab.in/resources
- M.Tech template archive: https://www.mustlab.in/_files/archives/87f6d3_6353534aa2f84650915e9d3e969c96f9.zip?dn=IIT-Kgp_MTP_Thesis_Template_LaTeX.zip
- General report template archive: https://www.mustlab.in/_files/archives/87f6d3_dff7ff0a6c4744ff8ce7196195fa1f5e.zip?dn=Report+Template.zip

The M.Tech template identifies itself as designed by MUST Lab, IIT Kharagpur. Its title page places the project title, student name and roll, supervisor, institute emblem, department and institute in a centred layout. The report adapts these conventions. `images/iitkgp_logo.jpg` is the unmodified `Images/iitkgplogo.jpg` asset from that archive.

The general report template is an article layout without an institutional cover. Its full formatting was not adopted. The final report retains A4 paper, 25 mm margins and readable 11-point body type, with a compact cover appropriate to BTP-1.

## Adaptations for this submission

The supervisor's supplied evaluation instructions take precedence: a 10–12-page report, a bona fide self-declaration on the first page, and spaces for student and supervisor signatures. The full thesis front matter, separate certificate page and degree-award wording were omitted. No signature or approval is generated.

This is an adaptation of a publicly shared IIT Kharagpur lab template. It is not represented as a prescribed E&ECE BTP-1 template or as institute approval of this report.

For comparison, the institute's official website https://www.iitkgp.ac.in/ was also inspected. Its header uses https://www.iitkgp.ac.in/assets/pages/images/logo.png. The standalone monochrome emblem supplied with the LaTeX template was chosen for print clarity.
